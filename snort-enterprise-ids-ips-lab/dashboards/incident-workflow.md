# SOC-Style Incident Workflow

## 1. Detect

Review Snort alert timestamp, source IP, destination IP, protocol, destination port, SID, and message.

## 2. Enrich

Determine whether the source is present in the local threat-intelligence blocklist and whether it is allowlisted.

## 3. Validate

Correlate the alert with packet captures and the test activity generated by the attacker VM.

## 4. Classify

Classify the event as reconnaissance, policy violation, suspicious network activity, or threat-intelligence match.

## 5. Contain

Use the validated IPS rule or blocklist entry to prevent further traffic.

## 6. Tune

Review false positives and adjust thresholds, ports, content matches, or allowlists.

## 7. Document

Record the alert, evidence, action taken, and final disposition.
